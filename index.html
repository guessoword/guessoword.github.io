What is Sixordle?

Sixordle takes the familiar word-guessing format — guess a five-letter word, get green and yellow feedback — and stacks six hidden words into a single puzzle, all checked against the same pool of guesses. It rewards the same letter-frequency instincts as any word game, plus a second skill: managing six boards' worth of information at once instead of one. A new puzzle is available daily, and there's no faster way to learn it than playing a round.

Why we build it this way

Guessoword is deliberately small. There's no account wall between you and the puzzle, no interstitial before the game loads, and no design decisions made to maximize time-on-page rather than the puzzle itself. That's a constraint we've kept since the studio started in 2022, and it's the reason people tend to come back daily rather than open the site once and bounce.
